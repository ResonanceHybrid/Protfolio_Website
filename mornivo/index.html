The Whole Idea

Why another calorie app? Because the existing ones make you do the work.

Calorie tracking usually goes like this: you eat something, open an app, search a database, scroll past forty kinds of "chicken breast, grilled", give up, and guess. By Thursday you've stopped logging entirely. The tracking was harder than the dieting.

Mornivo flips that. You take a photo of your meal and the AI figures out what's on the plate — calories, protein, carbs and fats, logged in seconds. Not near a camera-friendly moment? Say it or type it ("two eggs and toast") and it logs that too.

Everything else builds on that one habit: daily insights, weekly progress, macro distribution, water reminders, workouts that count toward your day. The app does the counting so you can just eat.

Quick Questions

The things people ask right before hitting download.

Is Mornivo free?

Yep — free to download on both the Play Store and the App Store.

How does the AI count calories from a photo?

You take a photo of your meal and the AI identifies what's on the plate, then estimates the calories, protein, carbs and fats for you.

Do I always need a photo to log a meal?

No — you can also log meals by voice or by typing them out, like "two eggs and toast".

What else does Mornivo track besides calories?

Water intake with reminders, exercise and calories burned, weight trends, plus daily and weekly history with macro insights.

What platforms is Mornivo available on?

Both — iOS via the App Store and Android via the Play Store.
